About Qahwa Craft

All of Saudi specialty coffee, in one place

Specialty coffee lovers in Saudi Arabia often buy beans and brewing equipment from several websites. That means many shipping fees and orders that arrive at different times.

Qahwa Craft brings products from local Saudi micro roasters and coffee tools into one shopping cart, with one checkout and one combined shipping fee. Our customizable subscriptions deliver freshly roasted coffee so you never have to reorder by hand.

Our goals

  • Combine the experience of buying coffee and equipment in one order and one shipment
  • Have a seamless, easy website with easy payment methods, fast loading and shipping
  • Offer an easy way to cancel subscriptions
  • Serve specialty coffee lovers and give local roasters a new sales channel

What comes next

  • Expanding to the other Gulf states within two years
  • Coffee subscriptions and supply for offices, companies and cafés within two years
  • Artificial intelligence that suggests coffee beans based on your preferences
  • More local, GCC and international roasters, exclusive products and gift boxes

Our vision

To be one of the leading online platforms for specialty coffee in Saudi Arabia, then expand to the GCC and the world, build our own specialty coffee roasting brand, and play a role in the global rise of Saudi specialty coffee.

What does Qahwa Craft sell?

Specialty coffee beans and custom blends from local Saudi micro roasters, brewing equipment such as manual and electric grinders, V60 kits, digital scales and paper filters, and customizable coffee subscriptions.

Can I order coffee and equipment together?

Yes. Add beans from several roasters and any equipment to one cart, pay once, and everything is delivered together in one box.

Why do I pay only one shipping fee?

Because your whole order ships as one combined delivery. You pay SAR 25 once per order, however many roasters you buy from. Subscription deliveries already include delivery.

Which payment methods do you accept?

mada and Apple Pay. With mada you enter your card details and complete your bank's verification. With Apple Pay you confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

How do monthly subscriptions work?

Choose a plan, your flavor profile (fruity, floral or chocolatey), your grind and your delivery schedule. We charge your mada card or Apple Pay before each delivery, and your next payment and delivery date are shown in My Account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to My Account and press Cancel next to the subscription. There are no cancellation fees and no minimum period. You can also pause it and resume later.

How do I track my order?

Open Track order and enter your order number and email, or press Track next to the order in My Account. You see each step from Placed to Delivered, and we tell you if a delay is expected.

How fresh is the coffee?

Every bag shows its roast date. Coffee tastes best from about 4 to 30 days after roasting.

Which grind should I choose?

Choose whole bean if you have a grinder. Otherwise pick pour-over (V60) for drippers, espresso for espresso machines, or French press for immersion brewers.

Can I return a product?

Yes, if it arrives damaged, incorrect or not as described. Contact us on WhatsApp, by email or through the contact form. See the Returns & refunds page for details.

Do I need an account to order?

Yes. An account keeps your orders, subscriptions and Qahwa Points in one place. It takes less than a minute.

Do you sell to offices and cafés?

Not yet. Subscription and supply plans for offices, companies and cafés are planned for the future. Send us a message and we will contact you when they launch.

This policy explains what information Qahwa Craft collects, why it is needed, and how it is used and protected.

  • What we collect: your name, phone number, email, delivery address, order history, subscription details and payment transaction information. We collect only what we need to provide our services.
  • Why: to process orders, arrange deliveries, manage subscriptions and contact you.
  • Sharing: we do not share your information with unrelated third parties unless there is a legal reason or you give consent. Only the delivery and payment services needed for your order receive it.
  • Access: only authorized staff can access customer data, and it is not used for unrelated purposes.
  • Protection: passwords are stored only as secure hashes, pages use HTTPS, and we never store full card numbers or security codes.
  • Your rights: you can view and correct your data in My Account and ask us to delete your account from the Contact page.
  • We follow the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).
  • Products and prices: product descriptions, prices and availability are shown on each product page. Prices are in Saudi riyals and include 15% VAT. The full cost, including the delivery fee, is shown before you pay.
  • Ordering and payment: add items to your cart, enter delivery details, choose mada or Apple Pay, and review your order and total. An order is confirmed once payment is approved, and you receive an electronic invoice.
  • Subscriptions: you choose the plan and delivery schedule. You are charged before each delivery, and your next billing date is shown in My Account.
  • Cancelling a subscription: cancel any time from My Account, with no fees.
  • Delivery: delivery arrangements and estimated times are explained on the Shipping page.
  • Your responsibilities: give a correct address and phone number, and keep your account password safe.
  • Intellectual property: the Qahwa Craft name, logo, product descriptions and website content belong to Qahwa Craft. Product images are used only with permission, and we respect the trademarks of our partner roasters and suppliers.
  • Customer rights: you have the right to correct information about products, prices and services, an electronic invoice, protection of your personal information, products that match their descriptions, updates about delivery and delays, cancellation, returns or refunds when eligible, and a way to contact us with complaints or questions.
  • These terms follow the laws of Saudi Arabia, including the E-Commerce Law, the PDPL and consumer protection requirements.
  • When you can ask: you can request a return, cancellation or refund if a product arrives damaged or incorrect, or does not match its description.
  • Coffee products: because coffee is a food product, returns also depend on the product condition and packaging and on the legal exceptions. Damaged coffee packaging or the wrong coffee is always covered.
  • How to ask: contact us on WhatsApp, by email or through the contact form with your order number and a photo if possible. We review the order, then replace the product, arrange a return or give an eligible refund.
  • Processing time: approved refunds go back to your original payment method (mada or Apple Pay) within 7–14 working days.
  • This policy follows your rights under the Saudi E-Commerce Law.
  • Areas: we deliver across Saudi Arabia.
  • One box, one fee: coffee and equipment from all roasters ship together as one combined delivery for SAR 25 per order. There are no extra charges. Subscription deliveries include delivery.
  • Estimated times: 1–3 working days in main cities and 3–5 working days in other areas.
  • Tracking: you get one tracking number and can follow your order on the Track order page.
  • Delays: we send you an update if a delay is expected.
  • Lost or damaged orders: contact us and we send a replacement or give a refund.
